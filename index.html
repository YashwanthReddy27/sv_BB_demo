Our approach

A plan built on what the film shows.

  1. 01

    Evaluate

    We start by watching the athlete play and shoot, so we know their strengths and where the gaps are before we set any goals.

  2. 02

    Train

    Focused, game-speed reps on the skills that matter most for that athlete, with a coach correcting form in real time.

  3. 03

    Film & analyse

    Every session is filmed. Software tracks the ball and the body, measuring release, arc, balance and more on every shot.

  4. 04

    Feedback & next steps

    Athletes and parents get a clear report: what went well, what to work on, and the drills for the next session.
